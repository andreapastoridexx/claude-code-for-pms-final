# 03 · Rewind — prompts

**Context:** You joined Rook two weeks ago as PM on Dispatch.
Release 4.2 shipped on 12 August, just before you arrived, and
landed badly.

Last session you read four conversations and every support ticket
since 4.2 — and found the two piles did not agree.

At the end of the session, type wrap up, and Claude Code saves the
prompts you wrote yourself below — not the starter prompt — updates
CLAUDE.md, and saves your work to GitHub. By Module 6 this file is a
prompt library built from your own questions.

---

### 1.

Have a group of respondents been missing calls more than others?

### 2.

How did the acceptance rate get impacted for these two groups?

### 3.

Whats the current missed % at?

### 4.

Compare the support_tickets table on rook-database against the pings table. Do they agree with each other? When did people start writing in, and when do the numbers actually move?

### 5.

You mention acceptance rate is back to ~70% as it was before 4.2. But missed % is still relatively high. How can this be?

### 6.

How has seasonality affected the numbers?

### 7.

Why might Vesper and Mite not have any tickets?

### 8.

Pick one responder from the file who went quiet. Show me every week for them, how many times we pinged them, how many they took. Then tell me what happened to that person, week by week, in plain English.

### 9.

What's Vesper's missed rate per week?

### 10.

Was Vesper affected by the routing changes?

### 11.

Was any of the "went quiet" respondents affected byt he reweight?
